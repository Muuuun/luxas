# Thermal conductivity of the CP-titanium rod

We need the thermal conductivity of the grade-2 titanium rod near 300 K for the cryostat support design. The steady-state heater series from the rod rig is in `input/steady_state.csv`; the rig is described in `input/README.txt`.

When you are done, also write `answer.json` in the project directory:
`{"kappa_W_per_mK": <value near 300 K>, "unc": <1-sigma uncertainty>}`
