# Period and damping of the new long pendulum

The exhibit sign for the new 2.4 m pendulum in the teaching hall needs its small-amplitude period and its amplitude decay time. The overhead-camera tracking of a one-hour run is in `input/track.csv`; the setup is in `input/README.txt`.

When you are done, also write `answer.json` in the project directory:
`{"period_s": <value>, "decay_time_s": <amplitude 1/e time>}`
