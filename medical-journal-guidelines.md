# Top 3 Medical Journal Formatting Requirements

> Reference compiled March 2026. Always verify against current author guidelines before submission.

---

## 1. New England Journal of Medicine (NEJM)

**Impact Factor:** ~176 | **Publisher:** Massachusetts Medical Society
**Author Center:** https://www.nejm.org/author-center/new-manuscripts

### 1.1 Article Types and Word Limits

| Article Type | Word Limit | Figures/Tables | References | Abstract |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Original Article | 2,700 | 5 total (combined figs+tables) | ~40 | 250 words, structured |
| Review Article | 2,700 | 5 total | ~40 | 150-250 words, unstructured |
| Special Article | 2,700 | 5 total | ~40 | 150-250 words, unstructured |
| Brief Report | Shorter (varies) | 2-3 total | Fewer | 150-250 words |
| Correspondence | 250 words (comment on published article); 500 words (new topic) | 1 figure or 1 table | 5 | None |

- Word count includes introduction through conclusion/discussion.
- Word count **excludes** abstract, figure legends, table notes, and references.
- Manuscripts far exceeding limits are returned unread.

### 1.2 Figure Specifications

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Max figures** | 5 (figures + tables combined) |
| **Preferred formats** | Vector: AI, EPS, SVG; also accepts PDF |
| **Bitmap formats** | TIFF, JPEG (for photographs) |
| **Resolution - Line art** | 1,200 DPI |
| **Resolution - Halftone/photos** | 300 DPI |
| **Resolution - Combination** | 600 DPI |
| **Color mode** | CMYK for print; RGB accepted for initial submission |
| **Submission method** | Insert into Word file (preferred) or upload separately |
| **Data visualizations** | Must be submitted as editable vector files (AI, EPS, SVG) |

- Each figure must include a title and legend on the same page.
- Graphs placed in PowerPoint/Excel/Word must be vector images, not rasterized.
- Additional figures may be submitted as online-only supplementary material.

### 1.3 Abstract Format

**Original Articles - Structured (250 words max):**
1. **Background**
2. **Methods**
3. **Results**
4. **Conclusions**

Requirements:
- Key findings must be reported as data, not unsupported statements.
- Clinical trials must include adverse events statement.
- Funding source and trial registration (registry name + ID) must appear at end of abstract.

**Other article types - Unstructured (150-250 words):**
- Should identify key points and basis of support.

### 1.4 Reference/Citation Style

- **Style:** Vancouver (numbered sequential)
- **In-text:** Arabic numerals in parentheses, numbered in order of first mention
- **Author format:** Last name + initials (no periods); list all authors if <=6; if >=7, list first 3 + "et al."
- **Journal abbreviations:** Index Medicus abbreviations
- **Format:** Author(s). Title. Journal Abbrev. Year;Volume:Pages.
- **Example:** `Ridker PM, Danielson E, Fonseca FAH, et al. Rosuvastatin to prevent vascular events. N Engl J Med 2008;359:2195-207.`
- Double-spaced, numbered consecutively.

### 1.5 Title Requirements

- No official character/word limit published, but titles should be concise and descriptive.
- For clinical trials: include the study/trial name.
- Running head: ~50 characters including spaces.

### 1.6 Required Sections and Compliance

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| **Ethics/IRB** | Statement of IRB/ethics committee approval required; written informed consent confirmation |
| **Clinical trial registration** | Required; registry name, trial ID, URL at end of abstract |
| **ICMJE COI disclosure** | All authors must complete ICMJE Disclosure of Interest form |
| **Data sharing statement** | Required for clinical trials (ICMJE requirement since July 2018): what data, when available, access criteria |
| **Funding** | Must declare all funding sources |
| **Author contributions** | ICMJE authorship criteria; all qualifying individuals must be named |
| **Statistical analysis** | NEJM statistical review; statistical methods section required |
| **Cover letter** | Optional |
| **Supplementary appendix** | Allowed for additional methods, data, figures |
| **Protocol & SAP** | Required for clinical trials (submitted with manuscript) |

### 1.7 Reporting Guidelines Required

| Study Type | Required Guideline |
|---|---|
| Randomized trials | **CONSORT** (flow diagram + checklist) |
| Observational studies | **STROBE** |
| Systematic reviews | **PRISMA** |
| Diagnostic accuracy | **STARD** |
| Quality improvement | **SQUIRE** |

### 1.8 Template Info

- **Preferred format:** Microsoft Word (.doc/.docx); also accepts .txt, .rtf, PDF, LaTeX
- **No official LaTeX template** provided by NEJM; third-party templates available (Overleaf, nextgenediting.com)
- **Word template:** Available from third-party sources (e.g., nextgenediting.com, Scribd)
- **Manuscript format:** Single file, double-spaced, 1-inch margins, tables at end of text file
- **Page numbers:** Consecutive, upper or lower right corner

## 2. The Lancet

**Impact Factor:** ~168 | **Publisher:** Elsevier
**Submission Guidelines:** https://www.thelancet.com/submission-guidelines
**Information for Authors (PDF, Feb 2026):** https://www.thelancet.com/pb-assets/Lancet/authors/tl-info-for-authors-1690986041530.pdf

### 2.1 Article Types and Word Limits

| Article Type | Word Limit | Figures/Tables | References |
|---|---|---|---|
| Article (Research) | 3,500 | 5 total (combined) | 30 |
| Article (RCT) | 3,000 (extendable to 4,500) | 5 total | 30 |
| Systematic Review/Meta-analysis | 3,500 | 5 total | Flexible (included studies + up to 30 others) |
| Seminar | 5,000 | Commissioned | ~100 |
| Series | 4,000 | Commissioned | ~100 |
| Comment | 1,000 | 1 | 8 |
| Correspondence (linked) | 250 | 1 | 5 |
| Correspondence (unlinked) | 400 | 1 | 5 |
| Viewpoint | 1,200 | 1 | 10 |

- Word counts exclude abstract, tables, figures, and references.
- Correspondence limited to 5 authors.
- Most Comments and Series are commissioned; spontaneous submissions are considered.

### 2.2 Figure Specifications

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Max figures** | 5 (figures + tables combined for research articles) |
| **Preferred formats** | EPS, AI, PDF (editable vector files) |
| **Fallback formats** | TIFF, JPEG (high-resolution) |
| **Resolution** | 300 DPI minimum (at final print size) |
| **Single column width** | 75 mm |
| **Double column width** | 154 mm |
| **Font in figures** | 10 pt Times New Roman Bold (headings); 10 pt (legends) |
| **Color** | Full color accepted; all figures redrawn by Lancet in-house illustrators |

- **Key:** All figures are redrawn into The Lancet house style by in-house illustrators. Submit clear, editable source files.
- If vector files are not available, supply high-resolution full-color JPEG or TIFF as last resort.
- Decimal points must be midline (23.4 rendered as 23**.**4).

### 2.3 Abstract Format

**Structured (300 words max) for Research Articles:**
1. **Background**
2. **Methods**
3. **Findings** (not "Results")
4. **Interpretation** (not "Conclusions")
5. **Funding**

**Systematic Reviews - Structured Summary (250 words max)**

Key notes:
- The Lancet uses "Findings" and "Interpretation" instead of "Results" and "Conclusions."
- Funding source must be stated within the abstract itself.

### 2.4 Reference/Citation Style

- **Style:** Vancouver (numbered sequential)
- **In-text:** Superscript Arabic numerals
- **Author format:** Surname + space + initials (no periods); list all if <=6; if >=7, list first 3 + "et al."
- **Format:** Author(s). Title. Journal Abbrev Year; Volume: Pages.
- **Numbered sequentially** in order of appearance in text.

### 2.5 Title Requirements

- **Research articles:** Descriptive, concise; no official word/character limit published.
- **RCTs:** Must be non-declamatory; must include the trial name/descriptor.
  - Example: *"Once-weekly dulaglutide versus once-daily liraglutide in patients with type 2 diabetes (AWARD-6): a randomised, open-label, phase 3, non-inferiority trial"*
- **Reviews:** Short and enticing, ideally <10 words.
- **Keywords:** 4-6 descriptive keywords required.

### 2.6 Required Sections and Compliance

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| **Ethics/IRB** | Institutional approval and informed consent confirmation required |
| **Clinical trial registration** | Required before enrollment begins |
| **ICMJE COI disclosure** | All authors complete ICMJE form; summary statement at end of text |
| **Data sharing statement** | Required; full data sharing encouraged |
| **Funding** | Declared in acknowledgments AND in abstract (Funding section) |
| **Author contributions** | Required; contribution of each author must be described |
| **Role of funding source** | Must state role of funder in study design, data collection, analysis, interpretation, writing |
| **Patient consent** | Written informed consent for identifiable patients |
| **Protocol submission** | Required for RCTs; observational studies should submit protocols |
| **Author statement forms** | Required at submission |

### 2.7 Reporting Guidelines Required

| Study Type | Required Guideline |
|---|---|
| Randomized trials | **CONSORT 2025** (checklist + flow diagram) |
| Observational studies | **STROBE** (submit with protocols) |
| Systematic reviews/Meta-analyses | **PRISMA** |
| Diagnostic accuracy | **STARD** |
| Quality improvement | **SQUIRE** |
| Animal studies | **ARRIVE** |

### 2.8 Template Info

- **Preferred format:** Microsoft Word (.doc/.docx); remove Endnote or other referencing software before submission.
- **LaTeX:** No official Lancet LaTeX template. Third-party available on GitHub (enphysoft/the-lancet-latex-template). Elsevier EES accepts LaTeX if bundled with a built PDF.
- **Figures:** Submit as separate editable files (EPS, PDF preferred).
- **Submission system:** Elsevier Editorial System (EES) at https://www.editorialmanager.com/thelancet/
- **Language:** English required. Translation services available for Chinese, Portuguese, Spanish authors.

## 3. JAMA (Journal of the American Medical Association)

**Impact Factor:** ~120 | **Publisher:** American Medical Association
**Instructions for Authors:** https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/pages/instructions-for-authors

### 3.1 Article Types and Word Limits

| Article Type | Word Limit | Figures/Tables | References | Abstract |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Original Investigation | 3,000 | 5 total | 50-75 | 350 words, structured |
| Systematic Review (without meta-analysis) | 3,000 | 5 total | 75 | 350 words, structured |
| Brief Report | 1,000-1,500 | 3 total | Fewer | Structured |
| Research Letter | 600 | 2 total | 6 | None |
| Viewpoint | 1,200-1,600 | 1-2 | 10-15 | None (Key Points instead) |
| Narrative Review (Clinical Review) | 3,000 | 4 total | 50 | Structured |
| Caring for the Critically Ill Patient | 3,000 | 5 total | 50 | Structured |
| Case Report/Case Series | 850 | 1-2 | 10 | Brief |

- Word counts exclude abstract, tables, figures, acknowledgments, references, and supplemental material.
- **Key Points** (3 bullet points): Required for Original Investigation, Systematic Review, Clinical Review, and other major article types.

### 3.2 Figure Specifications

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Max figures** | 5 total (figures + tables combined) for Original Investigation |
| **Accepted formats** | AI, BMP, DOC, EMF, EPS, JPG, PDF, PPT, PSD, TIF, WMF, XLS |
| **Color photos** | CMYK, TIF or JPG, 300 DPI, 5 inches width |
| **Line art** | 1,200 DPI |
| **Max file size** | 10 MB per file |
| **Preferred for final** | TIFF or EPS at publication resolution |

- Text, figures, and tables must be submitted as **separate files**.
- All figure parts should be clearly labeled (A, B, C, etc.).
- Microphotographs must include scale bars.

### 3.3 Abstract Format

**Structured (350 words max) for Original Investigations:**
1. **Importance** (unique to JAMA - why does this question matter?)
2. **Objective**
3. **Design, Setting, and Participants**
4. **Intervention(s)** or **Exposure(s)** (if applicable)
5. **Main Outcomes and Measures**
6. **Results**
7. **Conclusions and Relevance**

Additional requirements:
- Trial registration info (registry name, trial ID, URL) must be at end of abstract.
- JAMA's structured abstract is one of the most detailed in medical publishing.

**Research Letters:** No abstract required.
**Viewpoints:** No abstract; Key Points required instead.

### 3.4 Reference/Citation Style

- **Style:** AMA (American Medical Association) citation style - numbered sequential
- **In-text:** Superscript Arabic numerals
- **Author format:** Surname + initials (no periods); list all if <=6; if >=7, list first 3 + "et al."
- **Journal abbreviations:** Use MEDLINE/PubMed abbreviations
- **Format:** Author(s). Title. *Journal Abbrev*. Year;Volume(Issue):Pages. doi:XX
- **Example:** `Ridker PM, Danielson E, Fonseca FAH, et al. Rosuvastatin to prevent vascular events in men and women. N Engl J Med. 2008;359(21):2195-2207. doi:10.1056/NEJMoa0807646`
- DOIs are required when available.
- Follows AMA Manual of Style (Oxford Academic).

### 3.5 Title Requirements

- No strict character/word limit published, but conciseness is expected.
- For RCTs: include study name and design descriptor.
- Running head/short title: typically <=50-60 characters.

### 3.6 Required Sections and Compliance

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| **Ethics/IRB** | IRB/ethics committee approval statement required |
| **Informed consent** | Written informed consent confirmation required |
| **Clinical trial registration** | Required; info at end of abstract |
| **ICMJE COI disclosure** | All authors complete ICMJE form |
| **Data sharing statement** | Required (ICMJE policy) |
| **Funding/Support** | Listed under "Funding/Support" section |
| **Role of funder** | "Role of the Funder/Sponsor" section required |
| **Author contributions** | Listed at end of article |
| **Key Points** | 3 bullet points: Question, Findings, Meaning |
| **Statistical methods** | Must be described in sufficient detail |
| **Supplemental content** | Allowed; published online-only |

### 3.7 Reporting Guidelines Required

| Study Type | Required Guideline |
|---|---|
| Randomized trials | **CONSORT 2025** (checklist + flow diagram as supplementary file) |
| Observational studies | **STROBE** |
| Systematic reviews | **PRISMA** |
| Diagnostic accuracy | **STARD** |
| Quality improvement | **SQUIRE** |
| Animal studies | **ARRIVE** |
| Case reports | **CARE** |
| Qualitative research | **COREQ** or **SRQR** |

JAMA was instrumental in publishing the CONSORT 2025 update and strongly enforces reporting guideline compliance.

### 3.8 Template Info

- **Preferred format:** Microsoft Word
- **LaTeX:** Available via third-party services (Overleaf, Authorea, SciSpace/Typeset)
- **Word templates:** Available at nextgenediting.com and other template services
- **Citation management:** AMA style available in Zotero, Mendeley, EndNote, Paperpile
- **Submission system:** JAMA Network manuscript submission portal
- **AMA Manual of Style:** https://academic.oup.com/amamanualofstyle (authoritative reference)

## Shared Requirements (ICMJE Member Journals)

All three journals are ICMJE members and share these baseline requirements:

1. **ClinicalTrials.gov registration** (or equivalent registry) before first patient enrolled
2. **ICMJE Disclosure of Interest form** for all authors
3. **Data sharing statements** for clinical trials
4. **CONSORT/STROBE/PRISMA** compliance for applicable study types
5. **IRB/Ethics approval** statement
6. **Informed consent** statement
7. **ICMJE authorship criteria** (substantial contributions, drafting/revising, approval, accountability)
8. **No simultaneous submission** to multiple journals
