% Convention (figures v5, 208 level diagrams of the Luxas corpus): level lines, state labels and text are
% BLACK (83% / 88% of diagrams). A laser drive takes the colour of its wavelength (beam* below); its label is
% the same colour or black. A decay is a thin black wavy arrow. A level is coloured only when the colour names
% an identity the data figures also use (spin up/down, a species) — take it from report/figures/colorkey.json.
