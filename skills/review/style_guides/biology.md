# Biology Review Style Guide (Nature Reviews / Science house voice)

## Narrative voice

Biology reviews at the top tier occupy a register best described as pedagogical synthesis: warm enough to guide a specialist through unfamiliar sub-territory, confident enough to make evaluative claims, technically precise enough to sustain the trust of primary practitioners. Temperature is neutral to warm — never cold, rarely polemical. Paragraphs organize around claims about the biological phenomenon rather than around a single experiment. Person is first-plural when identifiable author-voices exist (the _we_ of "we review," "we discuss"), and third-person for consortium white papers or community assessments. New vocabulary is almost always followed immediately by an operational definition.

## Opening conventions

Biology reviews split between two dominant strategies. The historical-arc opener reaches back decades or centuries before naming the present as an inflection point: the HCA White Paper (Regev et al., eLife 2017) invokes 150 years of cell classification before naming massively parallel single-cell genomics; the microbiome review (Sepich-Poore et al., Science 2021) begins with the Ebers Papyrus (~1550 BCE) and Coley's 1890s bacterial experiments. The field-landscape opener names the technological frontier and identifies the gap within one to three sentences. First citations arrive between sentence three and five. A thesis sentence closes the opening paragraph in nearly all cases; biology reviews rarely open with a puzzle — they more commonly open with a prior success immediately qualified by its limitation.

## Section architecture

Specialist biology reviews carry six to ten top-level sections. Section title voice is predominantly gerund in Nature Reviews papers (Inferring cell histories from state manifolds) and nominal or full-statement in Science short reviews (CTLA-4 as a non-redundant immune checkpoint and clinical activity). An outlook or future-directions section is universal in Nature Reviews papers. Biology reviews invariably separate a technology section from an applications section, reflecting the field's persistent concern with whether the measurement tool perturbs the phenomenon it measures.

## Thesis-per-section convention

Top biology reviews open sections with claims about the biological phenomenon, not orientation sentences. "Unlike the state of a cell, the lineage history of a cell can be defined without the operational simplification that comes from reducing the dimensionality of thousands of measurements" — Wagner & Klein (Nat. Rev. Genet. 2020, §2), where the opening contrast is argument, not introduction. "The programmed death-1 (PD-1) receptor has emerged as a dominant negative regulator of anti-tumor T cell effector function when engaged by its ligand PD-L1, expressed on the surface of cells within a tumor" — Ribas & Wolchok (Science 2018, §3), where the emergence claim and the mechanistic specification precede any citation. "The RNA-guided nuclease activities of Cas13a and Cas12a have driven development of innovative tools for nucleic acid detection" — Knott & Doudna (Science 2018, §7), where the causal framing does interpretive work a summary sentence cannot.

## Synthesis moves

The canonical techniques in this corpus fall into five types.

The **state-versus-lineage contrast** is distinctive to biology reviews engaging method-level conflicts. Wagner & Klein (Nat. Rev. Genet. 2020) sustain the state-manifold / lineage-tree binary as the structural spine, enumerating seven failure modes of the two representations (delayed state divergence, false branch-points, state convergence, lineage heterochrony, gaps, false multipotency, hidden dynamics) as a numbered taxonomy in Fig. 2.

The **comparative platform table** manages citation-dense content. Wagner & Klein tabulate 12 lineage-barcoding technologies across editing system, barcode type, uniformity, and homoplasy; Stuart & Satija (Nat. Rev. Genet. 2019) tabulate 30+ single-cell methods. These tables permit claim-led prose to focus on interpretation rather than enumeration.

The **named relational framework** converts a scatter of findings into a conceptual unit: Sepich-Poore et al. (Science 2021) coin the "immuno-oncology-microbiome (IOM) axis" and reference it consistently; the HCA White Paper coins the "Sky Dive strategy" (uniform profiling → stratified → specialized), giving a protocol a name that makes it debatable.

The **running example with named variant** anchors abstract claims: Akdel et al. (Nat. Struct. Mol. Biol. 2022) return to the human DEGS1 protein across variant-effect, pocket, and model-building sections, making it a stress-test object for AlphaFold2's confidence architecture.

The **historical arc as causal argument** transforms prior failure into mechanistic prerequisite. Sepich-Poore et al. trace the rejection of the bacterial cancer theory to argue that the current microbiome renaissance requires orthogonal validation; Ribas & Wolchok trace Coley through cytokine therapies to argue that durable T cell immunity required the failure of non-specific stimulation first.

## Transition moves

Biology reviews navigate between sections through an explicit repertoire of connective gestures. "Unlike the state of a cell, the lineage history of a cell can be defined without the operational simplification that comes from reducing the dimensionality of thousands of measurements" — Wagner & Klein (Nat. Rev. Genet. 2020, §1→§2), contrast that reorients the reader's frame entirely. "It is this inherent programmability present in a diversity of naturally evolved systems that extends CRISPR-Cas applicability beyond precision genome editing" — Knott & Doudna (Science 2018, §2→§3), bridge. "Together with known causal mechanisms, the diversity of these 'complicit' mechanisms are notable, but they will require more rigorous experimentation and cross-cohort validation" — Sepich-Poore et al. (Science 2021, §1→§2), caveat. "High-confidence proteome-wide structural predictions open the door for a large expansion of predicted protein pockets" — Akdel et al. (Nat. Struct. Mol. Biol. 2022), zoom-out converting a specific capability into a research opportunity.

## Citation voice

Biology reviews are parenthetical-dominant: citations follow claim sentences rather than lead them. Paragraph density ranges from two to five citations per paragraph in short Science reviews to five to eight in Nature Reviews papers. Named citations in prose are reserved for landmark discoveries requiring attribution as a historical act (Allison's CTLA-4 hypothesis, Coley's bacterial toxins) and for the authors' own prior work used as a structural pivot. Named-author-in-prose fractions range from ~5% in tightly technical reviews to ~20% in those with historical-arc openings.

## Anti-stacking discipline

Top biology reviews open paragraphs with claims and weave authorship parenthetically. "Gut microbiota can regulate many functions of the tumor-bearing meta-organism, typically through immunomodulation" — Sepich-Poore et al. (Science 2021): the biological claim is categorical; experiments arrive as parenthetical citations. "Cas9 has proven to be a modular platform with functionally distinct DNA binding and nuclease activities" — Knott & Doudna (Science 2018): the architectural insight is the subject; the exploiting groups are parenthetical. The stacking construction ("Nejman et al. used quantitative PCR of 16S rRNA to estimate...") appears only when experimental design is itself the subject of evaluation. Claim-leading fractions in this corpus cluster around 45–60%.

## Figure and equation roles

Biology reviews carry no equations at the specialist scale. Figures serve five functions: the overview schematic maps conceptual architecture at the paper's opening; the conceptual framework diagram defines the relational structure being proposed (Fig. 1 of Wagner & Klein; Fig. 2 of Sepich-Poore et al.); the platform comparison figure provides empirical substrate for evaluative claims (Figs. 3–5 of Akdel et al.); the failure-mode taxonomy converts abstract warnings into a searchable reference (Fig. 2 of Wagner & Klein, panels a–h); and the timeline converts narrative argument into visual evidence of trajectory (Ribas & Wolchok, Fig. 2). Boxes appear in Nature Reviews papers at conceptual branch-points without interrupting the main argument; Science and eLife reviews carry none.

## Signature moves

1. Open with a temporal arc — 150 years, 4,000 years, a decade — that converts the present technological moment from novelty into historical resolution; the longer the arc, the stronger the claim that current methods represent a genuine inflection point.
2. Coin a named framework (Sky Dive strategy, IOM axis, state manifold, causal-vs-complicit taxonomy) and use it consistently; readers who adopt the terminology become advocates for the conceptual structure.
3. Anchor biological claims in a specific quantitative magnitude — pLDDT score, cell-density number, response-rate percentage — that can be contested; biology reviews gain authority by committing to a number, not by hedging.
4. Pair every application with an immediate failure case in the same section; the review earns evaluative authority by demonstrating it has looked for the limits of what it recommends.
5. Build the central conceptual tension as a named binary (state vs. lineage, causal vs. complicit, cellular branch vs. spatial branch, CTLA-4 vs. PD-1) and organize every subsequent section as an elaboration or resolution of that binary — the binary is the structural spine, not merely a pedagogical device.
