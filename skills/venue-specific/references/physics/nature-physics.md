# Nature Physics

> Official author guidelines: https://www.nature.com/nphys/submission-guidelines

Follows Nature Portfolio base rules (see `publishers/nature-portfolio.md`) with physics-specific additions.

## Article Types

| Type | Main Text Word Limit | Display Items | References | Notes |
|------|---------------------|---------------|------------|-------|
| **Article** | Up to 3,000 words | Up to 6 | ~50 | Excludes abstract, methods, refs, captions |
| **Article (longer)** | Up to 6,000 words | Up to 8 | ~100 | Extended studies |
| **Review** | Up to 6,000 words | Up to 8 | ~100 | Invited |
| **News & Views** | ~900 words | 1 | ~10 | Invited commentary |

## Formatting

### Abstract
- **Max 150 words**; no references

### Methods
- Online-only; max ~3,000 words
- No figures/tables (use Extended Data)

### Template
- Nature Portfolio templates apply
- **Initial submission: single PDF or Word file** — Nature does NOT require their LaTeX class at first submission, only at acceptance. For initial submission, plain `article` class with sane formatting is accepted.
- At acceptance: Nature accepts the manuscript as Word (preferred) or LaTeX. If LaTeX, use the official Nature class file (`nature.cls`) downloaded from their site. See `publishers/nature-portfolio.md` for the LaTeX template.

## Figures & Tables
- Same as Nature base: 89 mm single column, 183 mm double column
- 300 DPI minimum; vector preferred
- Sans-serif 5–7 pt; no "Scheme" numbering
- Extended Data: up to 10 items

## References & Citations
- Nature style: superscript numbered
- Article titles included; ≥6 authors → first + "et al."

## Physics-Specific Notes
- Equations should be numbered if referenced
- SI units preferred throughout
- Supplementary Information for detailed derivations
- Data Availability + Code Availability statements mandatory

## Common Pitfalls
- Same as Nature base: refs in abstract, Scheme numbering, figures in Methods
