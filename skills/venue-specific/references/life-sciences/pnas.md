# PNAS (Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences)

> Official author guidelines: https://www.pnas.org/author-center/submitting-your-manuscript

## Article Types

| Type | Word Limit | Display Items | Notes |
|------|-----------|---------------|-------|
| **Research Article** | 6 journal pages (~4,000 words + figures) | Up to 6 | Standard research |
| **Brief Report** | 4 journal pages (~3,000 words) | Up to 4 | Short communications |
| **Front Matter** | Varies | — | Commentaries, perspectives |
| **Colloquium Paper** | 6 pages | Up to 6 | From NAS colloquia |

## Formatting

### Title
- Max **100 characters** (including spaces)
- Avoid acronyms

### Abstract
- Max **250 words**
- Unstructured single paragraph

### Significance Statement
- **Required** — unique to PNAS
- **120 words** max
- Written for a broad scientific audience (non-specialist)
- Explains why the work is important

### Manuscript Format
- Word or LaTeX (PNAS template available)
- Double-spaced, 12 pt

## Figures & Tables

### Dimensions
- Single-column: **8.7 cm** (3.42 in)
- 1.5 column: **11.4 cm** (4.49 in)
- Double-column: **17.8 cm** (7.0 in)

### Resolution
- **300 DPI** minimum; **600 DPI** preferred for line art

### Formats
- TIFF, EPS, PDF preferred
- Color: RGB

### Font in Figures
- **6–8 pt**, sans-serif (Helvetica/Arial)

## References & Citations

### In-text Style
- Numbered in parentheses: (1), (2, 3), (4–6)
- Numbered in order of citation

### Reference List Format
- PNAS style (similar to Nature but with article titles)
- ≤5 authors: list all; ≥6: first author + "et al."
- Abbreviated journal names

## Required Sections
- **Significance Statement** — mandatory, 120 words
- **Data, Materials, and Software Availability**
- **Author Contributions**
- **Competing Interest Statement**
- **Classification**: Biological, Physical, or Social Sciences + specific subject area
- Keywords (3–5)

## Templates
- PNAS LaTeX template: https://www.pnas.org/author-center/submitting-your-manuscript
- `pnas.cls` available

## Common Pitfalls
- Missing Significance Statement (unique PNAS requirement)
- Significance Statement too technical (must be accessible)
- Exceeding 100-character title limit
