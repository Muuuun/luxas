# Science (AAAS)

> Official author guidelines: https://www.science.org/content/page/instructions-preparing-initial-manuscript

## Article Types

| Type | Word Limit (main text) | Display Items | References | Notes |
|------|----------------------|---------------|------------|-------|
| **Research Article (print)** | ~3,000 words | 3–5 | ~50 | ~5 printed pages |
| **Research Article (extended)** | ~6,000 words | Up to 6 | Up to 100 | Online; ~500-word print summary |
| **Report** (legacy) | ~2,500 words | Up to 4 | Max 30 | ~3 pages |
| **Review** (invited) | Up to 5,000 words | Varies | Varies | Excl. abstract, refs, captions |
| **Perspective** | ~1,800 words | 1 figure/table | Max 15 | — |
| **Technical Comment** | ~1,000 words | Max 2 | Limited | — |

## Formatting

### Title
- **Max 96 characters** (including spaces)

### One-Sentence Summary
- **Required** for all papers
- **Max 135 characters** (including spaces)

### Abstract
- **125 words** preferred; **250 words** maximum
- Unstructured (single paragraph, no headings)
- **No citations** in abstract
- No undefined abbreviations

### Font
- Body text: **Times New Roman**
- Figure labels: **Helvetica** (preferred) or other sans-serif

### Manuscript Format
- **Word .docx** preferred (single complete file with figures embedded)
- LaTeX accepted (Science-specific class file: `sciencemag.cls`)

## Figures & Tables

### Dimensions
| Width | Measurement |
|-------|------------|
| Single column | **5.7 cm** (2.24 in) |
| 1.5 columns | 12.1 cm (4.76 in) |
| Double column (full width) | **18.4 cm** (7.24 in) |

### Resolution
- All content: **300 DPI** minimum at final size

### Formats
- Preferred: **PDF, EPS, AI** (vector)
- Acceptable: TIFF (300 DPI)

### Color
- **RGB** (not CMYK)

### Font in Figures
- **Helvetica** preferred; ~7 pt (2.5 mm) at final size, minimum 5 pt

## References & Citations

### In-text Style
- Numbered sequentially in order of first citation
- Citation order: text first → figure/table legends → Supplementary Materials

### Reference List Format
- **List ALL authors** (no "et al." in reference list)
- Include **full article title**
- Journal names abbreviated per BIOSIS standards
- Include volume, first–last pages, year
- Example: `E. J. Neer, T. Kozasa, Sites for Ga binding on the G protein beta subunit. J. Biol. Chem. 273, 16265–16272 (1998).`

### Special Rules
- Do NOT use: op. cit., ibid., 3-m dashes, en dashes, or "et al." in reference entries
- Preprints: include `[Preprint]` label and DOI
- Datasets with DOI: must be cited in reference list

## Required Sections (in order)

1. Title
2. Authors + Affiliations (`*` for corresponding author)
3. Abstract
4. One-Sentence Summary
5. **Introduction**
6. **Results**
7. **Discussion**
8. **Materials and Methods** (brief in main; full in Supplementary)
9. **References and Notes**
10. **Acknowledgments**
11. **Funding** — complete grant numbers for all authors
12. **Author Contributions** — CRediT taxonomy encouraged
13. **Competing Interests** — or "no competing interests"
14. **Data and Materials Availability** — accession numbers, DOIs, repositories

### Additional Requirements
- **MDAR Checklist** — required for life sciences manuscripts
- **ORCID** — required for first and corresponding authors
- No co-first or co-senior author designations (use "contributed equally" footnote)

## Supplementary Materials
- Single file: **.docx or PDF**
- Max **50 MB** (main supplementary file)
- Auxiliary files: up to 10 files, total 25 MB
- Movie files: 50 MB each
- Numbering: fig. S1, table S1; references continue from main text

## Common Pitfalls
- Title exceeding 96 characters
- Missing One-Sentence Summary
- Citations in abstract
- Using "et al." in reference list (Science lists ALL authors)
- Forgetting MDAR checklist for life sciences papers
