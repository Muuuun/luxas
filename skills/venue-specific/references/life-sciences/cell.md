# Cell (Cell Press)

> Official author guidelines: https://www.cell.com/cell/information-for-authors

## Article Types

| Type | Word Limit | Display Items | Notes |
|------|-----------|---------------|-------|
| **Article** | ~7,000 words (incl. legends; excl. STAR Methods, supplemental, refs) | Max 7 (figures + tables) | Full-length primary research |
| **Resource** | Same as Article | Max 7 | Technical advances / databases |
| **Short Article** | 4,000 words (incl. title, authors, affiliations, summary, legends) | Max 4 | Concise research |
| **Review** | 5,000–8,000 words (incl. abstract) | 3–5 figures | Invited |
| **Perspective** | 4,000–5,500 words (incl. abstract) | Max 2 figures | Opinion |
| **Preview** | 1,000 words | 1 figure | Commentary on recent paper |

## Formatting

### Title
- **Max 150 characters** (including spaces)

### Summary (Abstract)
- **Max 150 words**, single paragraph
- No subheadings, no references, no abbreviations (unless widely known)

### eTOC Blurb ("In Brief")
- **Max 50 words**
- Third person: "First Author et al. ..."
- Plain language, no jargon

### Highlights
- **3–4 bullet points**
- Each bullet: **max 85 characters** (including spaces) — strict; returned if exceeded
- Results-oriented (not conclusions)
- Required for full research articles

### Graphical Abstract
- **1200 × 1200 pixels** (exact square) at **300 DPI**
- Font: **Arial, 12–16 pt**
- Formats: TIFF, PDF, or JPG (NOT PowerPoint)
- Single-panel; convey take-home message
- No numerical data, no eTOC text, no animal caricatures

### Manuscript Format
- **Word .docx** preferred (must be editable, not PDF)

## Figures & Tables

### Dimensions
| Width | Measurement |
|-------|------------|
| Single column | **85 mm** (8.5 cm) |
| 1.5 columns | 114 mm |
| Full page width | **174 mm** (17.4 cm) |
| Max figure size | 6.5 × 8 in (16.5 × 20 cm) |

### Resolution
| Content | Minimum DPI |
|---------|------------|
| Color / grayscale | **300 DPI** |
| Black and white | **500 DPI** |
| Line art | **1,000 DPI** |

### Formats
- Preferred: **TIFF, PDF**
- Also: EPS, JPG, PNG (for initial submission)

### Color
- **RGB** (not CMYK)

### Font in Figures
- **Helvetica or Arial** (sans-serif)
- **6–8 pt** at desired print size
- Panel labels: **capital letters** (A, B, C) in Helvetica/Arial

### Line Weights
- **0.5–1.5 pt** stroke width

## References & Citations

### In-text Style
- **Superscript numbered** (adopted October 2022, replacing Harvard)
- Numbered sequentially by first appearance

### Reference List Format
- Author(s). (Year). Article title. *Journal Abbreviation* Volume, Page range.
- "et al." only after **10 or more authors**
- Volume numbers in italics
- DOIs encouraged
- Example: `Mandavilli, A. (2007). Reinventing an industry. *Nature* 445, 138–139.`

## Required Sections (in order)

1. Title
2. Author list + Affiliations (Lead Contact designated)
3. Corresponding author email(s)
4. Summary (max 150 words)
5. Introduction
6. Results
7. Discussion
8. Resource Availability
9. Acknowledgments
10. Author Contributions
11. Declaration of Interests
12. References
13. **STAR Methods** (with Key Resources Table)
14. Figure Legends
15. Supplemental Information (index)

## STAR Methods

**No word limit.** Included online and in PDF.

### Required Headings (ALL CAPS):
1. **RESOURCE AVAILABILITY** — Lead Contact, Materials Availability, Data and Code Availability
2. **EXPERIMENTAL MODEL AND STUDY PARTICIPANT DETAILS**
3. **METHOD DETAILS**
4. **QUANTIFICATION AND STATISTICAL ANALYSIS**
5. **ADDITIONAL RESOURCES**

### Key Resources Table (KRT)
Three columns: REAGENT or RESOURCE | SOURCE | IDENTIFIER

12 resource categories: Antibodies, Bacterial/virus strains, Biological samples, Chemicals/peptides, Commercial assays, Deposited data, Cell lines, Organisms/strains, Oligonucleotides, Recombinant DNA, Software/algorithms, Other.

RRIDs highly recommended for antibodies and model organisms.

## Supplemental Information
- Primary: **Document S1** (PDF)
- Data files: Data S1, S2, etc. (non-proprietary, e.g., .csv)
- Each supplemental figure must fit on **a single page** (8.5 × 11 in)

## Common Pitfalls
- Highlights exceeding 85 characters per bullet (desk-rejected)
- Graphical abstract not exactly 1200×1200 pixels
- Using PowerPoint for graphical abstract
- Forgetting eTOC blurb
- Missing Key Resources Table in STAR Methods
- Using Harvard-style citations (Cell switched to numbered in 2022)
