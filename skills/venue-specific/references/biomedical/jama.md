# JAMA (Journal of the American Medical Association)

> Official author guidelines: https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/pages/instructions-for-authors

## Article Types

| Type | Word Limit | Display Items | References | Notes |
|------|-----------|---------------|------------|-------|
| **Original Investigation** | 3,000 words | 4 (figures + tables) | ~40 | Excludes abstract, refs |
| **Research Letter** | 600 words | 1 (figure or table) | ~6 | Brief findings |
| **Review** | 3,500 words | 5 | ~80 | Solicited |
| **Special Communication** | 3,000 words | 4 | ~40 | Policy/methodology |
| **Viewpoint** | 1,200 words | 1 | ~12 | Expert opinion |
| **Editorial** | 1,200 words | 0 | ~15 | Invited |

## Formatting

### Title
- No strict word limit stated; concise and specific

### Abstract
- **Structured** (unique to JAMA — most detailed structure of any major journal):
  - **Importance**
  - **Objective**
  - **Design, Setting, and Participants**
  - **Interventions** (if applicable)
  - **Main Outcomes and Measures**
  - **Results**
  - **Conclusions and Relevance**
- **Max 350 words**

### Manuscript Format
- Text, figures, and tables as **separate files**

## Figures & Tables

### Resolution
- Photographs: **300 DPI**
- Line art: **1,200 DPI**
- Combination: **600 DPI**

### Dimensions
- Photos/images: **5 inches** (12.7 cm) width preferred

### Formats
- Vector preferred (EPS, PDF, AI)
- Raster: TIFF, PNG

### Color
- Free color online; JAMA uses color in print

## References & Citations

### In-text Style
- **AMA style** — superscript Arabic numerals
- Numbered in order of citation

### Reference List Format
- AMA Manual of Style (11th edition)
- **DOIs required** for all references with DOIs
- ≤6 authors: list all; >6 authors: first 3 + "et al."
- Include article titles
- Standard journal abbreviations (Index Medicus)
- Example: `Smith JK, Jones AB, Wilson CD. Article title here. *JAMA*. 2024;331(5):400-408. doi:10.1001/jama.2024.xxxxx`

## Required Sections
- **Ethics / IRB approval** — mandatory
- **ICMJE Conflict of Interest form** — all authors
- **Data Sharing Statement** — mandatory
- **Funding**
- **Author contributions**
- **Trial registration** — mandatory for clinical trials
- **Protocol / SAP** — for clinical trials

### Reporting Guidelines
| Study Type | Required Guideline |
|------------|-------------------|
| RCT | CONSORT |
| Observational | STROBE |
| Systematic review | PRISMA |
| Diagnostic accuracy | STARD |
| Case reports | CARE |
| Quality improvement | SQUIRE |

## Templates
- Word preferred
- No official LaTeX template; third-party available on Overleaf

## Common Pitfalls
- Abstract not following JAMA's unique 7-section structure
- Missing DOIs in references (JAMA requires them)
- Text, figures, tables in a single file (JAMA wants them separate)
- Exceeding 350-word abstract limit
