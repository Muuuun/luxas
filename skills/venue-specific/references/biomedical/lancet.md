# The Lancet

> Official author guidelines: https://www.thelancet.com/information-for-authors

## Article Types

| Type | Word Limit | Display Items | References | Notes |
|------|-----------|---------------|------------|-------|
| **Research Article** | 3,500 words (RCTs: 3,000–4,500) | 5 (figures + tables) | ~30 | Excludes abstract, refs |
| **Review** | 3,500 words | 5 | 80–100 | Invited/solicited |
| **Seminar** | 5,000 words | 5–10 | 80–150 | Invited topic overview |
| **Series** | 3,500 words per paper | 5 | 60–80 | Thematic series |
| **Comment** | 800 words | 0 | 6 | Brief opinion |
| **Correspondence** | 250 words | 0 | 5 | Letters |
| **Viewpoint** | 2,000 words | 2 | 20 | Expert opinion |

## Formatting

### Title
- No strict word limit; **<10 words** recommended for reviews
- Non-declaratory titles preferred
- RCTs: include trial name/acronym and descriptor

### Abstract
- **Structured**: Background / Methods / Findings / Interpretation / Funding
- **Max 300 words**
- **Funding** is part of the structured abstract (unique to Lancet)

### Manuscript Format
- **Word** preferred; text as separate file
- Figures as separate editable files

## Figures & Tables

### Dimensions
- Single column: **75 mm**
- Full width: specific to Lancet layout

### Resolution
- **300 DPI** minimum

### Special
- **Lancet redraws all figures in-house** to match house style
- Provide source data files (Excel, AI, etc.)

### Color
- Free color

## References & Citations

### In-text Style
- **Vancouver style** — superscript numbers

### Reference List Format
- Numbered in order of citation
- ≤6 authors: list all; >6 authors: first 6 + "et al."
- Include article titles
- Standard journal abbreviations

## Required Sections
- **Ethics / IRB approval**
- **ICMJE Conflict of Interest form** — all authors
- **Data sharing statement** — mandatory
- **Funding**
- **Role of the funding source** — must state funder's role (or no role) in study
- **Author contributions** — CRediT encouraged
- **Trial registration** — mandatory for clinical trials
- **Protocol submission** — required for clinical trials

### Reporting Guidelines
| Study Type | Required Guideline |
|------------|-------------------|
| RCT | CONSORT |
| Observational | STROBE |
| Systematic review | PRISMA |
| Diagnostic accuracy | STARD |
| Meta-analysis | MOOSE |

## Templates
- Word preferred; no official LaTeX template

## Common Pitfalls
- Declaratory title ("X improves Y") — Lancet prefers neutral titles
- Forgetting to include Funding in structured abstract
- Missing "Role of the funding source" statement
- Not providing editable figure source files
