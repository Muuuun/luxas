# New England Journal of Medicine (NEJM)

> Official author guidelines: https://www.nejm.org/author-center/new-manuscripts

## Article Types

| Type | Word Limit | Display Items | References | Notes |
|------|-----------|---------------|------------|-------|
| **Original Article** | 2,700 words | 4–5 (figures + tables) | ~40 | Excludes abstract, refs |
| **Special Article** | 2,700 words | 4–5 | ~40 | Novel analyses |
| **Brief Report** | 1,200 words | 2 | 12–15 | Short findings |
| **Review Article** | 3,500 words | 4–5 | ~80 | Invited/solicited |
| **Perspective** | 1,200 words | 1 figure | ~10 | Opinion |
| **Case Records** | — | — | — | Massachusetts General Hospital cases |
| **Correspondence** | 250 words | 0 | 5 | Letters |

## Formatting

### Title
- No strict word limit stated; keep concise and specific

### Abstract
- **Structured**: Background / Methods / Results / Conclusions
- **Max 250 words**

### Manuscript Format
- **Word** preferred; single combined file
- Double-spaced, 12 pt

## Figures & Tables

### Resolution
- Photographs: **300 DPI**
- Line art: **1,200 DPI**
- Combination: **600 DPI**

### Formats
- Vector preferred (EPS, PDF, AI)
- Raster: TIFF, PNG

### Dimensions
- NEJM redraws most figures to house style; provide high-quality originals

### Color
- Free color online and in print

## References & Citations

### In-text Style
- **Vancouver style** — parenthetical numbers: (1), (2,3), (4–6)
- NOT superscript

### Reference List Format
- Numbered in order of citation
- ≤6 authors: list all; >6 authors: first 3 + "et al."
- Include article titles
- Standard journal abbreviations (Index Medicus)

## Required Sections
- **Ethics / IRB approval** — mandatory for all clinical/human research
- **ICMJE Conflict of Interest form** — mandatory for all authors
- **Data Sharing Statement** — mandatory
- **Funding disclosure**
- **Author contributions**
- **Trial registration** — required for all clinical trials (ClinicalTrials.gov etc.)
- **Protocol / Statistical Analysis Plan** — for clinical trials

### Reporting Guidelines
| Study Type | Required Guideline |
|------------|-------------------|
| RCT | CONSORT |
| Observational | STROBE |
| Systematic review | PRISMA |
| Diagnostic accuracy | STARD |
| Case reports | CARE |

## Templates
- Word preferred; no official LaTeX template
- Third-party LaTeX templates available (Overleaf, GitHub)

## Common Pitfalls
- Non-structured abstract
- Missing trial registration for clinical trials
- Missing ICMJE conflict of interest forms
- Exceeding 2,700-word limit for Original Articles
