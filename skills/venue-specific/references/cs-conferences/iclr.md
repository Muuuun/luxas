# ICLR

> Official author guidelines: https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2026/AuthorGuide

## Article Types

| Type | Page Limit | Camera-Ready Extra | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------------------|-------|
| **Main conference paper** | 9 pages | +1 page | Excludes refs and appendix |

## Formatting

### Page / Layout
- Per `iclr2026_conference.sty`
- References and appendix unlimited

### Template
- **iclr2026_conference.sty** — mandatory
- `math_commands.tex` included for common math macros

### Abstract
- No strict word limit

## References & Citations
- Numbered style
- Natbib recommended

## Required Sections
- **LLM disclosure** — mandatory (must disclose use of LLMs)
- **Blind review** — double-blind
- **Reciprocal reviewing** encouraged
- **Reproducibility** section recommended

## Common Pitfalls
- Missing LLM disclosure statement
- Exceeding 9-page limit
