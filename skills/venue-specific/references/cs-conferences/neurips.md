# NeurIPS

> Official author guidelines: https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2025/CallForPapers

## Article Types

| Type | Page Limit | Camera-Ready Extra | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------------------|-------|
| **Main conference paper** | 9 pages | +0 | Excludes refs and appendix |

## Formatting

### Page / Layout
- Single column for review; camera-ready format per `neurips.sty`
- **References do not count** toward page limit
- **Appendix unlimited** but reviewers not required to read

### Font
- Defined by `neurips.sty` — do not override

### Abstract
- No strict word limit; keep concise (~200 words typical)

### Title
- No strict word limit

### Template
- **neurips.sty** — mandatory
- LaTeX strongly preferred

## Figures & Tables
- Vector graphics (PDF) for plots; PNG 600 DPI for photographs
- Colorblind-safe palettes recommended
- Captions below figures, above tables
- Referenced in text before appearing

## References & Citations
- Numbered style: [1], [2–4]
- Natbib recommended: `\citet{}` for textual, `\citep{}` for parenthetical

## Required Sections
- **Mandatory checklist** (reproducibility, broader impact)
- **Lay summary** required post-acceptance
- **Blind review** — anonymize all submissions
- **Code submission** encouraged (but not mandatory)
- **Broader Impact Statement**

## Common Pitfalls
- Exceeding 9-page limit (instant desk rejection)
- Self-identifying language in blind submission
- Not filling out the mandatory checklist
- Forgetting broader impact statement
