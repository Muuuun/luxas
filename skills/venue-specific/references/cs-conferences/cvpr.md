# CVPR / ICCV / ECCV

> Official author guidelines: https://cvpr.thecvf.com/Conferences/2026/AuthorGuidelines

## Article Types

| Type | Page Limit | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| **Main conference paper** | 8 pages + references | Appendix in supplementary |

## Formatting

### Page / Layout
- Two-column layout
- **Double-blind** review
- Supplementary video allowed

### Template
- CVPR LaTeX template — mandatory

### Abstract
- No strict word limit; keep concise

## Figures & Tables
- **High-quality visualizations** expected (this is a vision conference)
- Comparison figures (side-by-side) standard
- Qualitative + quantitative results both expected
- Vector graphics preferred

## References & Citations
- Numbered style: [1], [2]

## Required Sections
- **Double-blind** — remove all identifying information
- Supplementary materials (video demonstrations encouraged)
- Quantitative comparison tables with SOTA

## Common Pitfalls
- Poor quality visualizations (reviewers weigh visual results heavily)
- Missing qualitative comparison figures
- Self-identifying references ("In our previous work [X]...")
- Supplementary exceeding reasonable size
