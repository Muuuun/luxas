# ICML

> Official author guidelines: https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026/AuthorInstructions

## Article Types

| Type | Page Limit | Camera-Ready Extra | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------------------|-------|
| **Main conference paper** | 8 pages | +1 page | Excludes refs and appendix |

## Formatting

### Page / Layout
- Two-column layout per `icml2026.sty`
- References and appendix do not count toward page limit

### Template
- **icml2026.sty** — mandatory

### Abstract
- No strict word limit; typically ~150–200 words

## Figures & Tables
- Vector graphics (PDF) for plots
- Captions below figures, above tables
- booktabs style for tables

## References & Citations
- Numbered style
- Natbib: `\citet{}` / `\citep{}`

## Required Sections
- **Broader Impact Statement** — mandatory
- **Blind review** — double-blind
- **Reproducibility checklist**

## Common Pitfalls
- Exceeding 8-page limit
- Not accounting for the +1 page camera-ready allowance when planning content
