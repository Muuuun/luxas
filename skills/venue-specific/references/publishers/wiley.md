# Wiley (Base Rules)

> Official author guidelines: https://authorservices.wiley.com/author-resources/Journal-Authors/Prepare/index.html

Applies to Wiley journals (Angewandte, Adv. Mater., Adv. Funct. Mater., Small, etc.).

## Formatting

### Page / Layout
- Varies by journal; most use Wiley templates
- Double-spaced, 12 pt for review manuscripts

### Abstract
- Typically **200 words** max (varies by journal)

### Keywords
- 3–7 keywords required

## Figures & Tables

### Dimensions
- Single-column: **~8.4 cm**
- Double-column: **~17.8 cm**

### Resolution
- **300 DPI** minimum; 1000 DPI for bitmap line art

### Color
- **CMYK** preferred for print (especially Angewandte)
- RGB acceptable for online-only

### Formats
- TIFF, EPS, PDF, PNG

## References & Citations
- Varies by journal:
  - Angewandte: superscript [brackets], no article titles
  - Adv. Mater.: non-superscript [brackets], full article titles
- Check specific journal file

## Required Sections
- Author Contributions (CRediT) — increasingly required
- Supporting Information
- ToC entry (for many Wiley journals)

## Templates
- Wiley author services: https://authorservices.wiley.com/author-resources/Journal-Authors/Prepare/index.html
- **Some journals (e.g., Angewandte) mandate their specific template**

## Common Pitfalls
- Using a generic template when the journal mandates its own
- RGB figures when CMYK is required
- Not checking journal-specific citation style (varies within Wiley)
