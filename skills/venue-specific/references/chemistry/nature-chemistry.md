# Nature Chemistry

> Official author guidelines: https://www.nature.com/nchem/submission-guidelines

## Article Types

| Type | Word Limit (main text) | Display Items | References | Notes |
|------|----------------------|---------------|------------|-------|
| **Article** | Up to 3,000 words (excl. abstract, methods, refs, captions) | Up to 6 figures/tables | ~50 | Standard research; 3–8 printed pages |
| **Article (longer)** | Up to 6,000 words | Up to 8 figures/tables/boxes | ~100 | Extended studies |
| **Review** | Up to 6,000 words | Up to 8 display items | ~100 | Invited |
| **Perspective** | 300–800 words | 1 display item | Up to 15 | Focused commentary |

## Formatting

### Abstract
- Max **150 words**
- **No references allowed** in the abstract

### Title
- No explicit word limit; Nature journals favor short, punchy titles

### Methods
- Max **3,000 words**
- Cannot contain figures or tables (use Extended Data or SI instead)
- Subdivide with topical subheadings

## Figures & Tables

### Dimensions
- **Single-column**: 89 mm
- **Double-column (max)**: 180 mm

### Resolution
- **300 DPI** minimum

### Formats
- TIFF, EPS, AI, PDF (vector preferred for data figures)

### Font in Figures
- **5–7 pt** sans-serif; Symbol font for Greek characters

### Chemical Structures
- ChemDraw (.cdx at 100%), using the **Nature Chemistry ChemDraw template**

### Special Rules
- **No "Scheme" numbering** — use "Figure" for all display items
- Use **scale bars** (not magnification factors)
- Include **error bars** where appropriate
- **Extended Data**: up to 10 additional figures (online only, peer-reviewed)

## References & Citations

### In-text Style
- Superscript Arabic numerals

### Reference List Format
- Numbered in order of citation
- Surname first, then comma, then initials
- **Article titles required** (upright text, not italic; first word capitalized)
- If >5 authors: first author + "et al."
- Nature reference style

## Required Sections
- Online Methods (detailed, published online)
- Extended Data (up to 10 figures/tables, peer-reviewed, online only)
- Supplementary Information (additional data)
- **Data Availability Statement** — required
- **Code Availability Statement** — required if applicable
- **Author Contributions** — required (CRediT-style)
- **Competing Interests** — declaration required

## Templates
- No mandatory template for initial submission
- Single file (Word or PDF, up to 30 MB) with text + figures together
- LaTeX and Word templates available for revised/accepted manuscripts

## Common Pitfalls
- Using "Scheme" instead of "Figure" (Nature Chemistry doesn't use Scheme numbering)
- Putting figures/tables in the Methods section (not allowed)
- References in the abstract
- Exceeding 150-word abstract limit
- Not using the Nature Chemistry ChemDraw template for structures
