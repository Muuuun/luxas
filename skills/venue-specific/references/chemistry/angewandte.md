# Angewandte Chemie International Edition

> Official author guidelines: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/15213773/homepage/notice-to-authors

## Article Types

| Type | Limit | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| **Communication** | ~2,500 words (abstract → conclusion; excludes refs); **4 printed pages max** | Flagship; returned if >4 pages |
| **Research Article** | 5,000 words (abstract → conclusion; excludes refs); 6–10 pages | Full studies |
| **Review** | 100,000 characters (with spaces) | Invited |
| **Minireview** | 40,000 characters (with spaces) | Invited |
| **Highlight** | 8,500 characters; max 2 pages | Third-person commentary |
| **Essay** | Variable | Opinion / thought-piece |

## Formatting

### Abstract
- Max **200 words**

### Keywords
- **5 keywords** required

### Title
- No explicit word limit; concise and informative

## Figures & Tables

### Dimensions
- **Single-column**: 8.4 cm
- **Double-column**: 17.8 cm

### Resolution
- Color / photographs: **300 DPI** minimum
- Bitmap stick diagrams: **1000 DPI**

### Formats
- Raster: TIFF, JPG, PNG
- Vector: EPS
- Embed in Word via "Insert Graphic"

### Color
- **CMYK** (not RGB) for print figures

### Font in Figures
- **7–8 pt** (1.5–2 mm tall)

### Cover Image
- Circular, 16.5 cm diameter, 300 DPI TIFF, CMYK

## References & Citations

### In-text Style
- Superscript numbers in square brackets after punctuation: [1], [1,2], [1–4]
- **One citation per reference number** (no sub-parts like [1a], [1b])

### Reference List Format
- Numbered in square brackets at end of text
- **Article titles are NOT included**

## Required Sections
- Supporting Information (SI) — separate upload, published online
- Author Contributions — CRediT (Contributor Roles Taxonomy) statement
- Dedication — optional

## Templates
- **Must use Angewandte template** — submissions using ACS or generic templates are returned
- Word and LaTeX: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/15213773/homepage/notice-to-authors

## Common Pitfalls
- Using ACS or generic template instead of the Angewandte template (desk rejection)
- Communication exceeding 4 printed pages
- Figures in RGB instead of CMYK
- Including article titles in references (Angewandte omits them)
- Using sub-parts in reference numbers ([1a], [1b])
