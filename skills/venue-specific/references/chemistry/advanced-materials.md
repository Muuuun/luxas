# Advanced Materials (Wiley)

> Official author guidelines: https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/hub/journal/15214095/author-guidelines

## Article Types

| Type | Word Limit | Display Items | Notes |
|------|-----------|---------------|-------|
| **Communication** | 2,000–5,000 words | 1 display item | Short rapid-dissemination |
| **Research Article** | 3,000–8,000 words (entire ms) | 3–8 display items | Standard full paper |
| **Progress Report** | 20–30 pages | 15–20 display items | Critical field overview |
| **Review** | 15–20 pages (excl. refs, display items, captions) | 9–12 display items | Comprehensive |
| **Research News** | 4–5 pages | Up to 3 display items | Non-specialist audience |

## Formatting

### Font
- **12 pt** in manuscript; standard fonts in figures
- Double-spaced

### Abstract
- Max **200 words**

### Keywords
- **3–7 keywords** required

### Title
- No explicit word limit; concise

## Figures & Tables

### Dimensions
- **Single-column**: 8.5 cm (3.35 in)
- **Double-column**: 17.8 cm (7.01 in)

### Resolution
- **300 DPI** minimum (1000 pixels wide at single-column width)

### Formats
- Raster: TIFF, PNG (photographs, micrographs)
- Vector: EPS, WMF (plots, graphs, chemical structures)

### Tables
- Max **3 horizontal rules** (head, neck, foot)
- Avoid 1–2 column tables

### TOC Entry
- Required: short text (**50–60 words**, third person, general audience) + ToC graphic

## References & Citations

### In-text Style
- Numbered in square brackets, **non-superscript**: [1], [1,2]

### Reference List Format
- Numbered in order of appearance
- Include all authors (if >6: list 3 + "et al.")
- **Include full article title and full journal name**
- DOI optional but recommended
- No strict formatting at submission — typesetters reformat upon acceptance

## Required Sections
- Table of Contents (ToC) entry (text + graphic)
- Supporting Information — separate upload
- Experimental / Methods section — required if new results presented
- Structure: Introduction → Main Text → Conclusion → (optional) Experimental

## Templates
- MSWord template: https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/hub/journal/15214095/author-guidelines
- Non-template: 12 pt font, double-spaced

## Common Pitfalls
- Missing ToC entry (text + graphic both required)
- Tables with vertical lines or more than 3 horizontal rules
- Forgetting the Experimental section when reporting new results
