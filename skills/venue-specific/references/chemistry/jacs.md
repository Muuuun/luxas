# Journal of the American Chemical Society (JACS)

> Official author guidelines: https://pubs.acs.org/page/jacsat/submission/authors.html

## Article Types

| Type | Word Limit | Display Items | Notes |
|------|-----------|---------------|-------|
| **Communication** | 2,200 words (abstract → end of main text; excludes refs) | ~5 graphics | Flagship short format |
| **Article** | No strict word limit | No strict limit | 8–10 journal pages; returned if exceeded |
| **Perspective** | 9,000 words (abstract + main text + figure captions) | — | Invited; contact Editor first |
| **Spotlight** | Short invited commentary | — | ~2 pages, editor-invited |

## Formatting

### Page / Layout
- **Paper size**: US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) — **not A4**
- Double-spaced for review manuscripts

### Font
- Figures: **Arial or Helvetica**, minimum **8 pt**

### Abstract
- Max **250 words**
- Unstructured (no section labels)

### Title
- No explicit word limit; keep concise

## Figures & Tables

### Dimensions
- **Single-column**: 3.25 in (8.25 cm)
- **Double-column**: 7.0 in (17.8 cm)

### Resolution
- Color / photographs: **300 DPI** minimum
- Grayscale / line art: **600 DPI**

### Formats
- TIFF (preferred), PDF, EPS, CDX (ChemDraw)

### Color
- RGB accepted

### TOC Graphic
- **Required** for Communications and Articles
- Visual summary of the paper

## References & Citations

### In-text Style
- Superscript Arabic numerals, numbered consecutively in order of appearance
- Multiple refs: comma-separated (e.g., ¹·²); sequential ranges with dash (e.g., ¹⁻³)

### Reference List Format
- Numbered; **includes article titles**
- Minimum info: author, abbreviated journal title, year, DOI
- ACS Style Guide

## Required Sections
- TOC Graphic
- Supporting Information (SI) — separate upload
- ORCID — required for corresponding author

## Templates
- Word and LaTeX templates: https://pubs.acs.org/page/jacsat/submission/jacsat_templates.html

## Common Pitfalls
- Using A4 instead of US Letter paper size
- Exceeding 8–10 pages for Articles (desk-rejected)
- Missing TOC graphic
- Figures below 300 DPI
- Using fonts other than Arial/Helvetica in figures
